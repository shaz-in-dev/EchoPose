# ADR-0001: Product Direction and Differentiation

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-04-11

## Context

EchoPose is compared against larger WiFi sensing projects with broader ecosystems.
Competing directly on repository vanity metrics is not viable in the short term.

## Decision

EchoPose will differentiate by:
- Operations-first deployment paths (Compose + k8s + systemd).
- Uncertainty-aware sensing outputs and domain-shift monitoring.
- Tactical and situational analytics integrated with pose stream.
- Benchmark-backed claims with reproducible scripts.

## Consequences

Positive:
- Clear and defensible identity.
- Strong roadmap from research modules to production features.

Negative:
- Requires disciplined benchmark and docs investment before growth metrics.
- Feature delivery may be slower than demo-heavy approaches.
