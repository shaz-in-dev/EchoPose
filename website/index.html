The technology

Radio waves are
the new eyes.

Three small sensor nodes read Channel State Information — the way WiFi bends, reflects and shifts as a human body moves through it. Our AI engine reconstructs a live 17-point skeletal pose from radio alone, at 20 measurements per second.

64 subcarriers / node 20 Hz sensing rate 17 joint skeleton 3 node mesh edge inference · <40 ms
Privacy first

It even works
through walls.

Radio doesn't need line of sight — and it can't take a photograph. EchoPose senses motion and posture through ordinary interior walls while remaining physically incapable of capturing a face, a screen, or an image of any kind. Raw signal data is processed on-device and discarded.
