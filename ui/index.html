Smart Home Setup

Pair EchoPose with Apple Home, Google Home, or Amazon Alexa using the Matter protocol. Scan once — pairing is permanent.

Scan in Apple Home / Google Home / Alexa app
Manual code:
✓ Commissioned
EchoPose is paired with your smart home platform
Open in Browser

Requires MATTER_ENABLED=true in your .env and Node.js ≥18.

EchoPose auto-discovers in Home Assistant via MQTT. All sensors appear automatically — no YAML needed.

Add to your .env:
HA_MQTT_BROKER=192.168.1.10
HA_MQTT_PORT=1883
HA_MQTT_USERNAME=   # optional
HA_MQTT_PASSWORD=   # optional
Sensors published:
  • Presence (binary_sensor)
  • Person Count, Heart Rate, Respiratory Rate, SpO2
  • Activity, Gait Speed, Gesture, Sleep Stage
  • Stress Score, Fall Detected (expires 30 s), Anomaly
  • Health Alert
